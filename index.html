FAQ

Common questions

Do you fabricate ducts in-house?

Yes. Square and round ducting is cut and formed in our own workshop, so we can match drawings and site measurements precisely.

How often should a kitchen hood be cleaned?

It depends on cooking volume. Many busy kitchens schedule monthly or quarterly cleaning. We recommend a site visit to set the right frequency.

Will you provide a written quote?

Yes. After the free site visit you receive a written scope with line-item pricing and photos of the area.

Do you work outside office hours?

Yes. Night and weekend slots are available for restaurants and factories, especially on maintenance plans.

What areas do you cover?

We serve the full Klang Valley area including Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Shah Alam, Cheras, Ampang, Kepong, and surrounding Selangor districts.

Do you handle emergency call-outs?

Yes. Emergency response is available 24/7 for clients on a maintenance plan. Standalone emergency call-outs are available subject to crew availability.

What affects the price?

Duct length, the number of hoods, access and working hours, and how heavily the system is soiled. Your written quote lists each item separately after the free site visit.

Will I get documents for compliance or insurance?

Yes. Every job ends with a photo report and service log. Enterprise clients can request additional compliance documentation.
